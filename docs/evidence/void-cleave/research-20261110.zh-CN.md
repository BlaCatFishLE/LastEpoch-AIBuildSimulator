Ignite inflicted by Void Cleave gains additional fire penetration based on your increased void damage.  Void Cleave gains the '{'fire'}' tag. |

Void resistance shred chance from all sources is converted to fire resistance shred and effects related to void resistance shred now depend on fire resistance shred instead.  Void Cleave loses the '{'void'}' tag. |

## 实现缺口

- 当前主技能选择含 Hellish Chasm：基础虚空伤害全部转为火焰，不能直接按技能名称做虚空伤害计算。
- Time Rot、Bleed、目标高血量、Critical Vulnerability、Armor Shred 与冷却均影响本案例，需要独立规则和状态/时序口径。
- 当前 FR hit 输入适配器具有 FR/Spellblade 专有节点映射及 Buff；不能只改 ability_id 复用。
- 技能模板、快照适配器、运算、共享页面/serve、独立预期均未实现；当前 research 模式只完成数据首接。
